Find free courses that actually work for you.

Fikelela helps learners discover free skills-training courses that match their accessibility, device and data needs — before they waste time or data on a course they can't actually use. We don't host courses ourselves. We help you choose the right one, first time.

Free and open isn't always usable

Thousands of free tech courses exist on major platforms. But for a learner using a screen reader, on a low data budget, or who needs captions, "free" doesn't always mean "usable."

Platform accessibility ≠ content accessibility

A platform can be well-built and still host individual courses that rely entirely on undescribed video, with no transcript or caption in sight.

No one tells you which ones actually work

You can filter free courses by price, duration or language on most course directories — just not by whether they'll work with your screen reader, your data plan, or your device.

How Fikelela helps

We sit on top of the free courses already out there, and only surface the ones that match what you actually need.

1

Tell us what you need

Search by topic or skill, then filter by captions, transcripts, screen-reader support, keyboard navigation, low-data availability or downloadable content.

2

See a real Access Profile

Each sample profile shows the findings a real review would record — not just a title and a star rating.

3

Choose with confidence

Compare a few options side by side, then go to the course on the provider's own site — no guesswork, no wasted data.

Illustrative now, manually verified in production

The Access Profiles in this demo use clearly labelled sample findings to show how Fikelela would work. A production directory would publish findings only after real checks, include the review date and invite learners to report incorrect or outdated information.
